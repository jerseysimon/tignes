Good to know

  • Sleeping 8: only Les Soldanelles, Les Lodges des Alpages and the Belle Plagne flat are sold for 8. Le France has beds for 8 but is sold for 7, and Edelweiss is sold for 6–8. The rest sleep 7, so one of us is on a squeeze.
  • Just outside 300 m: Squaw Valley in Plagne Villages (63 m² duplex, sleeps 8, 3 bathrooms, €3,869 on Ski-Planet) is about 360 m from the Plagne Villages meeting point.
  • Also free and within 300 m, all in Plagne Centre or Plagne Villages on Ski-Planet: Cerro Torre 45 m², sleeps 7, €3,149 (no lift) · Le France 42 m², sleeps 7, €3,549 · Le France 54 m² with 3 bedrooms, sleeps 7, €3,929 · Le Pelvoux, sleeps 5–7, €4,199 · La Cordillère 68 m², sleeps 7, €4,669 · Les Aollets 68 m², sleeps 7, €4,669 · Mont Soleil 53 m², sleeps 7, €4,809.
  • Which ski school: the meeting points on the map are ESF's. Each village has its own group-lesson meeting point and a Piou Piou club for the youngest. Oxygène also meets on the Plagne Centre snow front. Check which meeting point your lesson uses when you book.
  • The villages: Plagne Centre is the hub with the most shops and lifts. Plagne Villages and Plagne Soleil sit just above it at 2,050 m and are quieter and ski-in/ski-out. Plagne 1800 is lower and chalet-style. Belle Plagne is the prettiest and highest, and the dearest.
  • Extra costs: on most Ski-Planet flats, bed linen, towels and end-of-stay cleaning are optional extras. Ski-Planet charges 30% to cancel more than 30 days out and 100% after that.
  • Lift passes: a 6-day La Plagne pass is €370 per adult and €296 per child (up to 12), so €2,664 for all 8. Adding Les Arcs (Paradiski) makes it €2,968.
  • Prices and availability change daily, so check the listing before booking.
