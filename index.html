Good to know

  • Saturday to Saturday only. Nothing sleeping 8 was free for a Sunday 27 Dec start on any site checked.
  • Flats for 7: none free that week. Last year's flat (Grand Tichot A39, Val Claret) is already booked for 26 Dec. It's free the week after (2–9 Jan) at €2,300.
  • Extra costs: Tignes Réservation adds €20 per booking plus tourist tax (€1.10 per adult per night last year). Linen is extra unless the listing says it's included, and a damage deposit is taken on arrival.
  • Paying and cancelling (Tignes Réservation, last year's terms): 30% to book, balance 30 days before arrival. Cancelling more than 60 days out (before about 27 Oct) cost €15–50.
  • The areas: Val Claret is the top end, by the Grande Motte funicular. Le Lac is the main village by the lake, with the Tovière gondola and Palafour chair. Le Lavachet is quieter, just past Le Lac. Free shuttle buses link them in winter.
  • Also free, but dearer for what you get: Appartement Prestige 4 Pièces, Val Claret, 60 m², sleeps 8, €7,636 on Booking.com · Appartement Pixel, Les Almes (Le Lac), 80 m², €7,983 on Booking.com. Other flats for 8 that came up were dearer still.
  • Prices and availability change daily, so check the listing before booking.

Where they are

What stands out

  • How the totals work: the cheapest single flat found for the week plus 6-day lift passes at 2026/27 prices for 4 adults and 4 kids, assuming all four kids get child rates. Transfers, ski hire, lessons and Tignes' family-pass discount aren't included.
  • Les 2 Alpes is cheapest overall, mostly because its passes cost about €1,470 less than Tignes'. The glacier keeps the top reliable, but the village is lower (1,650 m), so the runs home are the weak spot in a lean December. The flat is a new Airbnb listing with one bathroom and no reviews yet. Buying the passes as a family pack saves another €160.
  • La Plagne is the closest like-for-like (see the La Plagne flats): similar height, a bigger linked area and the same Geneva road as Tignes. The €2,819 flat sleeps 7; the cheapest that sleeps 8 is €3,869.
  • Cervinia is high, with a glacier and cheaper passes. Strong wind can close the top lifts, and the drive from Geneva goes through the Mont Blanc tunnel.
  • Not worth it this week: Val Thorens (only 3 flats for 8 left, from €7,777), Les Menuires (from €4,601) and La Rosière (nothing for 8 under €7,210).
  • 6-day passes, adult / child: Tignes–Val d'Isère €468 / €378 · Les 2 Alpes €266.50 / €213 · La Plagne €370 / €296 · Paradiski €412 / €330 · Cervinia €338 / €236.50 · Les Arcs €368 / €295 · Alpe d'Huez €336.50 / €264 · 3 Vallées €421 / €345 · Val Thorens–Orelle €367.50 / €301. Child age bands vary by resort (mostly up to 12; up to 17 in the 3 Vallées).
  • Drive times are rough and assume no traffic. Saturday traffic over Christmas can add hours.
